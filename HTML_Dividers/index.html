My Poem to You

Alfred Kenneth Reidich


I envied the way

you could light up someone's face,

the way you could so easily be so friendly,

so nice, even when you should have been mean.


Your eyes were so pretty,

as bright as a star,

wasn't I blind

to lack the conscience

to see how beautiful you are


A mind painted like art;

ideas so divine,

so relentless:

a fiery intention

who dared

to match your style?


I wish that I could read this poem to you

and stare at your bright blue eyes

just one more time

but who am I but a jester

as life

is to live,

or to die.
